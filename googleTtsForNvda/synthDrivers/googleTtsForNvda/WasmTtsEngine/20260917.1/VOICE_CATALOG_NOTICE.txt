The voice package catalog in voices.json was generated and extended by the
Google TTS For NVDA developers from voice package metadata extracted from the

Source APK SHA-256:
93294E46CD4975E21E0F11C80882B97A21E529F38C36A841480F81B85520517F

The repository-root generate_voices_json.py tool normalizes, merges,
deduplicates, and sorts the source catalog. For new packages, it may download
the corresponding .zvoice archive from the URL in the source metadata and read
backend_pipeline.textproto to obtain speaker identifiers and gender metadata.
It also generates localized display names and package dependency relationships.

The resulting catalog records package identifiers, download URLs, checksums,
compressed sizes, dependency information, and speaker metadata. Downloaded
.zvoice voice packages are not distributed as part of this add-on.
